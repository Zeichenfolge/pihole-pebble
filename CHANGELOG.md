# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10-09

First release.

- Status screen: active / paused with countdown, queries and blocked queries (24 h),
  active clients, blocklist size, last list update
- Pause for 30 s, 5 min, 15 min, 1 h or indefinitely; resume at any time
- Hold SELECT or use Quick Launch to toggle with the default pause
- Vibration when a pause ends, even with the app closed
- Recently blocked domains with client, time, count and reason; allow and undo allow
- App glance in the app list
- Touch support on Pebble Time 2
- German and English, demo mode (`demo` as address)
